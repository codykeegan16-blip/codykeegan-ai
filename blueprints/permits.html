Public Records to B2B Subscription

Every building permit filed is an early signal. Operators pay for early signals.

The hook: Every building permit is an early signal. Operators pay for early signals.

What it is

Every county clerk in America publishes the same boring data feed: building permits, business licenses, contractor registrations. It updates daily. It's free. Almost no operator reads it.

But that data is gold to anyone who sells to construction adjacents — roofers, landscapers, fencing companies, AC techs, security installers. A new pool permit means a pool builder is about to need help. A new commercial buildout means a flooring contractor has a window.

A few people figured this out and built six-figure businesses selling it back as a subscription. There's room for a thousand more in different verticals.

How the workflow runs

  1. A scraper pulls each county permit feed daily — most are CSV exports or simple API endpoints.
  2. A language model categorizes each permit (residential vs commercial, scope, ZIP, value bracket) and tags by which trades would care about it.
  3. A simple subscription page sells access by vertical: "roofers $97/mo, plumbers $97/mo, AC techs $97/mo" — one county, one trade, one price.
  4. Email digest goes out each morning to subscribers: "Here are the 23 permits in your county and trade this week. Reach out to these owners."
  5. Operators in the trade pay willingly because the alternative is door-knocking blind.

Why this works now

AI categorization makes the raw permit data usable in minutes instead of hours. Subscription tooling makes monetization trivial. The data has always been there. The packaging is the new piece.

Operator Filter take

Operator Filter says yes. It's recurring revenue, high-margin, low-competition because the buyers don't know to ask for it. The defensibility comes from being first in a county-trade pair. Stack 50 county-trade combinations and you have a real business.

What you'd need

  • A scraper that handles diverse county data formats
  • AI categorization (any modern language model)
  • A subscription/billing layer
  • An email digest delivery system
  • One county to start (yours), one trade to start (the one you understand best)
