The Idea

Most AI tool lists are 30 tools long and useless. This is the OPPOSITE — the small list of tools that actually do all the work across three live businesses (photo booth, GHL agency, real estate system). Every line item has a reason. Every cost is real. Tools that were tried and dropped are also listed so you don't repeat the experiment.

The wedge: Three businesses on AI. Total stack cost ~$144/mo. That's less than most operators pay for one SaaS subscription that does one thing.

How AI Runs It — 5 Steps

  1. Start with GHL — It replaces 10 SaaS tools (CRM, email, SMS, automation, scheduling, forms, pipelines, calendars, websites, funnels). One bill.
  2. Add Claude Pro — The thinking layer. Claude.md template (Blueprint 01) makes the output sound like you across every business.
  3. Add Blotato — Distribution. 9 platforms scheduled from one place + AI video generation when you can't film.
  4. Add the API tier — OpenAI gpt-4o-mini for workflows that need programmatic AI (lead scoring, content scoring, etc.).
  5. Layer in voice (ElevenLabs + Vapi) — Optional but high-leverage. ElevenLabs for content voiceover, Vapi for inbound calls.

Path To Revenue

Replace 3-5 SaaS tools with this stack. Tool spend goes down. Output goes up.

The Math

Equivalent SaaS stack to do the same work without GHL bundling: $1,200-2,000/mo. This stack does the same work for $144/mo. Net savings: $13K-22K/year per business.

You do: Set up once per business. ~10 hours of build per business after the first one.

AI does: Every recurring operational task — lead capture, follow-up, scheduling, content distribution, customer support, voice answering.

Why Now (2026)

  • GHL's automation depth in 2026 covers 90% of small-business operational needs. The remaining 10% is direct API integration.
  • Stacking tools across multiple businesses spreads the fixed cost — the marginal new-business cost approaches zero.
  • The affiliate revenue from GHL alone (40% recurring) pays for the entire stack within 3-5 referrals.

The tools are commoditized. The advantage is knowing which ones, in which order, doing what. That's the stack.
