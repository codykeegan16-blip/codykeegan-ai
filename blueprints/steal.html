The Idea

Your competitors are training your sales scripts for you in public. Every 1-star review is a customer saying exactly what they wish was different — in their own words. Stack 50 of those reviews, feed them to Claude with the Steal Prompt, and out comes 5 patterns: what customers hate, what they wish for, what closes them. Use those exact phrases in your outreach and demos.

The wedge: Most operators guess at positioning. Your competitors' angry customers wrote it for you. Free.

How AI Runs It — 5 Steps

  1. Pick the competitor — Direct competitor or category leader. Both work.
  2. Copy 50 reviews — Mix of 1-star, 2-star, 5-star. The 1-stars tell you what customers HATE. The 5-stars tell you what they LOVE. Both matter.
  3. Paste into Claude with the Steal Prompt — The template structures the analysis: extract sales-script patterns, identify hidden objections, find positioning gaps.
  4. Get 5 patterns back — Each one in customer language. Examples: "I wish they answered the phone", "too pushy", "never explained what's included."
  5. Drop those exact phrases into your demos and outreach — Customer words close. Industry jargon doesn't.
