Court Filings to Lawyer Alerts

Every new lawsuit is an early signal. IP attorneys pay for early signals.

The hook: Every new trademark application is a deal an attorney could close.

What it is

PACER publishes every federal court filing the day it's filed. The USPTO publishes new trademark applications. State courts publish civil filings. Bankruptcy filings are public. Tax-lien filings are public.

Attorneys would pay good money to know which of their existing clients just got sued, which competitors just filed for trademark infringement, which of their lapsed leads is about to get foreclosed on. They don't have time to scan the feeds themselves.

Build the scan. Sell the alerts.

How the workflow runs

  1. A scraper pulls daily feeds from PACER, USPTO TESS, state court electronic-filing systems, county tax-lien indexes.
  2. A language model categorizes each filing by type, parties involved, jurisdiction, dollar amount where applicable.
  3. Subscribers (attorneys, paralegals, B2B sales teams) tell the system what to flag — "trademark applications in skincare," "any filing involving these 200 companies," "new corporate formations in Texas."
  4. A daily email digest fires at 7 AM with their matched alerts.
  5. Plus a real-time webhook option for premium subscribers ($297 vs $97 a month).

Why this works now

Court and government data has been public forever. AI now lets one operator package it into something attorneys actually use. The buyers (lawyers, corporate dev teams, B2B sales orgs at companies with high CAC) are pre-qualified to pay subscription pricing.

Operator Filter take

Operator Filter: this is a subscription business with a built-in moat (the categorization model trained on your customers' actual matches gets better over time). Stickiness comes from the alert habit — once an attorney opens your digest with their morning coffee for 60 days, switching cost is huge.

What you'd need

  • Feed access (most are free; PACER charges per page)
  • AI categorization and entity recognition
  • A subscription billing layer
  • A daily-email + real-time-webhook delivery system
  • One niche to launch (e.g., trademark watch in your state) so you can prove the model before expanding
