Brand Bible Template

AI writes generic slop unless you teach it your voice. The Brand Bible Template is the file every operator needs before writing a single word with AI.

The Idea

Most operators try to use AI for content and quit within a month because the output sounds like every other AI post. The fix isn't a better prompt — it's a structured Brand Bible file the AI reads BEFORE every task. Voice rules, do/don't list, signature phrases, 10 real examples of how you actually talk. Once you have the file, every prompt returns output that sounds like you wrote it.

The wedge: The difference between AI that sounds like you and AI that sounds like an intern with a thesaurus is one file.

How AI Runs It — 5 Steps

  1. Download the template — Grab the markdown file (link below). Standardized structure: identity, voice rules, do/don't, example outputs.
  2. Fill in your voice rules — What words you use, what you never say, what tone you favor. Be specific (e.g., 'never say synergy', 'always end emails with a question').
  3. Add 10 real examples — Paste 10 of your actual posts, emails, or DMs. The AI learns by example far better than by description.
  4. Save as CLAUDE.md in your project — Drop the filled-out file at the root of any AI workspace you use. Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT projects — all of them respect this convention.
  5. Reference it in every prompt — First line of every prompt: "Read CLAUDE.md and apply the voice rules." The output is now you.
